# 3m-bayonet-valve
Flow control valve compatible with the 3M Bayonet respirator filter connector. 

Due to needs of my own, I've made a set of flow control valves for the 3M Bayonet connector! 
These units snap into all 3m Bayonet system connections where filters would otherwise go. They
enable control over the amount of airflow moving across the bayonet connector and are *great* if
you have any needs involving controlling the respiratory flow of those to whom said respirators
are attached to :3

This design comes in two flavors: right-hand locking and left-hand locking. The interior valve 
control always operates on a righty-tighty lefty-loosey basis, so the only difference is how the
entire unit attaches to and detaches from the bayonet connector. The LH variant locks in with a 
left-hand (counterclockwise) turn and the RH variant locks in with a right-hand (clockwise) turn. 
This means that the LH model will tend to want to unlock when the valve gets loosened, and the RH
model will tend to want to unlock when the valve gets tightened. Thus the RH model will be safer
on the tighten and the LH will be easier to loosen in an emergency. Keep these in mind as you 
decide which one to use!
